# Developing secure professional communications and strengthening trust in network expertise across ISRM

**Proposal for discussion · 28 October 2026**

*Proposed application capabilities, delivery stages and budget limits*

## Trust is a mandate in trusted consultancy

Professional confidence is earned through experience, sound judgement and evidence that can be checked. Timely access to reliable information helps people decide whose expertise to draw upon, what requires further investigation and where professional review is needed.

ISRM brings together practitioners, chapters and specialist knowledge. The proposal is to give that community a practical digital means of finding relevant expertise, reviewing information and working with colleagues. Our contribution combines management consulting, professional research and application development around ISRM’s requirements.

## What you would be able to do

The proposed core application would allow authorised users to:

- Find people and organisations through agreed fields, filters and an explorable network graph.
- Read a short profile or fuller dossier, see the sources behind a record and identify information that still requires review.
- Assemble named working lists and shortlist suitable people for a defined enquiry or activity.
- Maintain agreed fields, correct records and add supported relationships, with appropriate access and review arrangements.
- Request AI-assisted OSINT research and pass sourced findings to a professional reviewer, where that connection and its terms have been agreed.
- Use agreed connections to ISRM’s existing systems for communication, learning, knowledge resources or project work.

A shared category in the graph is a route for exploration. It does not establish a personal introduction, endorsement, willingness to participate or permission to contact someone. AI-assisted research can contain errors and requires professional review before reliance.

## A core application, with two possible workstreams

**Core: a practical foundation for professional interaction.** Search, dossiers, working lists, evidence records and agreed access arrangements would provide a common foundation for subsequent services. The existing OSINT research assistant would require an agreed connection to the application. Research volumes, source permissions and ongoing AI/API costs would be defined separately.

**Engagement and acquisition.** A possible next workstream would help ISRM turn appropriate interest into participation: membership, learning enquiries, partnerships, client enquiries, introductions and referrals. It would organise agreed recurring actions and follow-up around those outcomes. Audience size alone does not establish reachable people, demand, conversion or revenue.

**Developing and sharing expertise.** A further workstream could help turn professional contributions into reviewed resources, discussions, research, learning and services for members or clients. An AI assistant trained or configured to support consultants with professional knowledge would be a separate future proposal, requiring agreement on purpose, rights, evidence and evaluation.

A **workflow** is a repeatable sequence of actions, responsibilities and decisions organised around a useful result. Automation can support the sequence; professional judgement remains with the appropriate people.

Work on either optional workstream could begin when ISRM decides it is needed, provided the necessary core capabilities and dependencies are in place. Each would have its own agreed scope, independently useful results and acceptance criteria. Neither is included in the current core estimate merely because it is selected for discussion.

## Delivering the core

1. **Specification and agreement:** confirm the users, functions, data, integrations, responsibilities and acceptance criteria; agree the detailed proposal and delivery plan.
2. **Initial deployment and integration:** provide access to an agreed test group, connect the agreed systems and collect practical feedback.
3. **Refinement and handover:** address agreed findings, complete the acceptance process and settle the agreed code, licence and support arrangements.

*Provisional payment terms shown in the scope are 30% / 50% / 20%, subject to agreement. These terms currently concern the core only. They do not define the terms of the optional workstreams.*

The current budget position and simpler existing-application option are set out in the [scope and budget review](/scope). This overview does not introduce a second price list or an additional charge for the graph.

## What the demonstration establishes

The demonstration lets you explore a supplied sample, inspect records and service descriptions, assemble a shortlist and prepare a request for a detailed proposal. Draft choices are held in the browser; they are not a signed agreement and are not sent automatically.

It is not evidence of completed production integrations, live OSINT research, verified personal relationships or permission to use identifiable information. Before operational use, the parties would agree the purpose and permitted data, responsibilities, access arrangements, source review, correction and retention processes. The demonstration should not receive confidential client information.

Try the application with a familiar enquiry. Identify the capabilities that would help your work, note any gaps and review the proposed scope and budget. We can then prepare a detailed proposal for the selected requirements and discuss any further development separately.
