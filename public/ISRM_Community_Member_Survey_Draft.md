# Your experience of working with ISRM

**Discussion draft · 28 October 2026 · Demonstration only; no responses are collected**

This proposed opening survey has eight questions. The original longer questionnaire remains a working source for more detailed conversations. Completion time has not been measured; it should be checked with a small test group before invitation wording is agreed.

## Proposed introduction for members

We would welcome your views on how ISRM could make it easier to find relevant expertise, work with colleagues and use the knowledge held across the network.

Please tell us what would be useful in your own work, what currently gets in the way and how you might wish to contribute. You do not need to support the proposed application to take part; views about what is unnecessary are equally welcome.

Participation would be voluntary. Please do not include confidential client or employer information, sensitive security details, or other people’s contact details. Interest expressed here would not publish a profile, verify expertise, commit you to a project or subscribe you to further messages.

*Before opening this survey, ISRM would provide the responsible organisation, purpose and use of responses, relevant privacy information, contact route and any necessary permissions. This review version does not collect responses.*

## Optional follow-up, kept separate from the main survey

After responding, a member could choose to request a conversation, review their own profile or discuss participation in a pilot. Any contact form would explain who would contact them, for what purpose and through which channel. It would not be preselected, and declining would not prevent completion of the main survey.

Do not describe responses as anonymous until the actual platform, metadata and any possible link to contact details have been reviewed.

## Further discussion with the Board or interested members

The longer source questionnaire can support a separate discussion about strategic expertise gaps, chapter priorities, knowledge ownership and access, contribution terms, safeguards for any future index, and the possible role of an AI assistant for consultants. These subjects should not all be required in the opening member survey.

## Decisions needed before launch

- Confirm the survey owner, responsible organisation, purpose and intended recipients of findings.
- Agree the final questions and test their clarity, length and accessibility with a small group.
- Agree the platform, privacy information, data access, retention, correction and contact arrangements.
- Agree the invitation, authorised sender and distribution channel.
- Test the form, optional follow-up route and completion acknowledgement before sending invitations.

Report actual responses and their limits. A response, stated interest, permission for contact, verified expertise and actual participation are distinct events. Do not treat expressed interest as a forecast of paid membership, client work or revenue.
