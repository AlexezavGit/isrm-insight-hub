# Introduction

![Status: In progress](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-In%20progress-yellow)

Implement the internal partner-facing, light-theme review surface at `/partner` for the ISRM expert-search prototype, proposed pilot functions, project-wide scope, cost scenarios, partner inputs, and client/board decisions. Keep review drafts browser-local and clearly distinguish source data, derived values, and assumptions. The intended review is a closed partner circle: names and professional mini-dossiers supplied for this purpose may be visible to authorized reviewers, but contact details and raw evidence are not part of a mini-dossier. The current local-only demonstration does not authenticate partners and is not a partner-access link. Do not expose it externally until an invitation/authentication and authorization boundary is implemented and reviewed. The client-facing `/proposal` is a separate concise offer page; the main `/` workspace retains expert search and provides an opt-in service-dependency view inside the member cabinet.

## 1. Requirements & Constraints

- **REQ-001**: The `/partner` route must provide two pages. Page one switches between expert search and service/function configuration without losing filters or selections; page two contains order confirmation and review.
- **REQ-002**: Expert search may show names, organization/role and source-linked professional mini-dossier details only for the closed, authorized partner review, because the client provided the records for this purpose. Exclude direct contact details and raw evidence from mini-dossiers. Do not suppress aggregate counts; clearly state that a match is not evidence of interest, consent, qualification, or contact permission.
- **REQ-003**: The closed-circle review is not public access. The current partner preview is pseudonymized and local-only; it does not yet deliver the requested names-bearing, source-linked mini-dossier review. Do not send a partner URL or expose names until a reviewed authentication and authorization boundary exists. Describe pseudonyms as pseudonymization with residual re-identification risk, not anonymization or de-identification.
- **REQ-004**: The Trust & Influence Index (TII) must be presented as a proposed co-design deliverable, not a validated or sector-wide standard. Decisions, evidence rules, reviewer selection, training, and validation remain distinct work packages. Use the TII label consistently in active UI and client-facing documents.
- **REQ-005**: The function configurator must use a fixed radial layout: co-design sessions at the center, MVP functions in the first ring, pilot functions in the second ring, and later functions in the third ring. Selecting a function adds potential expert nodes linked by the CRM-sector map and current search filters. Label this mapping as an assumption, not evidence of interest, consent, qualification, or likely adoption. Expose each function's purpose, dependencies, pilot role, measurable KPI definition, impact pathway, phase, and evidence/assumption status.
- **REQ-006**: The review/order view must include project purpose, proposed pilot, scope selection, partner/client comments, rate inputs, requested changes captured in template fields (user/role, need, expected behavior, outcome, data/integration, and acceptance criterion), supporting-source links, and a browser-local save/export path. It must not claim that a submission was transmitted or approved.
- **REQ-007**: The project-cost panel must compare direct software-provider cost, separately priced partner work plus confirmed external costs, and an equivalent-scope boutique all-in quote. Cost formulas must be visible; avoid double-counting partner and supplier management.
- **REQ-008**: Software-provider market comparators must retain their units, country, source period, and comparator class. Clutch US and Ukraine company-hour bands are directory benchmarks, not quotes or national floors/ceilings. Do not impute a UK company-hour rate when the cited country-level source says unknown; show UK contractor day-rate comparators separately and state any hour/day conversion.
- **REQ-009**: Do not invent hours, rates, demo-use fees, dossier timings or external costs to make a total appear complete. Use an evidenced input, an explicitly labeled and editable planning assumption, or TBD. A blank/unknown input must never calculate as zero or be presented as a confirmed price. Show formulas, units, source and assumptions; distinguish measured effort, partner-entered values, planning assumptions, supplier quotes and excluded costs. Show project-wide variance only against an agreed numeric whole-project reference with matching scope, currency and period; otherwise show TBD.
- **REQ-010**: Explain at the start that partners review first and follow the same configuration path later used by the client; completing the configurator means reviewing or approving the proposed function set. Define partner responsibilities as functions and outputs that can transition to standard management after the pilot. Describe connected services and integrations as proposed architecture unless implementation is verified.
- **REQ-011**: Present four internal partner scopes: (1) consulting/strategy beyond included outputs; (2) application MVP; (3) optional expert-community cycle after MVP, including proposed TII co-design, agenda-based working groups, report/articles, mention tracking and a conference-readiness assessment; and (4) later public-product or multi-community continuation. The client-facing `/proposal` presents two priced planning stages (standalone application MVP and optional expert-community cycle) and describes additional consulting separately without double-billing TII work included in the expert cycle. Show the derived low/base/high scenarios with formulas, units, work-package links, and explicit unverified-rate/planning-assumption labels; do not present them as supplier quotes or final contract prices. These scopes are not navigation steps or low/base/high price tiers. Paid membership, payment processing, AI, multi-tenancy and public release are not implied MVP or expert-cycle requirements; include only by separate selection, specification, approval and pricing.
- **REQ-013**: Explain why each scope exists, its concrete outputs, acceptance evidence and exclusions. Consulting defines the need and decision model; MVP makes the approved workflow repeatable; the pilot tests that workflow with one client and reports evidence/limitations; post-pilot scope is decided from those results. Do not promise participation, conversion, expert availability, impact or ROI.
- **REQ-012**: Provide an EN/UK language switch on the proposal and document panel so embedded explanations, table headings, and partner-facing content can be reviewed in either language. Keep the partner-role rate fields editable, show the fee recalculated for each responsibility, and keep the selected-scope project total visible while the long budget table is being edited. State that this demonstration does not authenticate a reviewer or restrict edits to one role.
- **SEC-001**: The current preview contains pseudonyms and broad sectors only and remains local-only. It does not fulfill the names-bearing mini-dossier review. Names and professional mini-dossier fields may be enabled only after reviewed, authenticated and authorized access; exclude direct contact details and raw evidence. Do not deploy or send a partner link before access review; keep the home route's existing behavior unchanged.
- **SEC-002**: Keep candidate selections in memory only; do not persist candidate IDs or aliases in browser-local drafts. Migrate and purge previously saved candidate selections while retaining unrelated draft fields. Label drafts and exports as local and non-submitted.
- **CON-001**: Keep both graph canvases dark and every other partner-review surface—including the right-side mini-dossier and order view—light. Preserve the existing home search, filters, graph and unrelated routes; add only an opt-in dependency graph inside the existing member cabinet.
- **CON-002**: Do not introduce a backend, persistence claim, third-party tracking, or new dependency.
- **GUD-001**: Use source-linked, auditable formulas and label every unsourced estimate `ASSUMPTION`; distinguish observations, calculations, assumptions, and partner-entered values.
- **GUD-002**: Keep controls keyboard accessible, use visible focus, responsive layouts, reduced-motion support, non-color status cues, and a high-contrast option.
- **PAT-001**: Follow the existing TanStack Start server-function, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and route patterns.
- **PAT-002**: Keep non-graph partner-review surfaces in the route's existing light palette and reserve the dark petrol treatment for graph canvases. Use labels and shapes as well as color to convey meaning; do not import visual or content assets from other projects.
- **CON-003**: Keep the task, data, sources, and design decisions within the ISRM project; exclude materials belonging to other projects.
- **REQ-014**: Make `/proposal` directly answer what the client will be offered next, use static interface thumbnails derived from actual application capabilities, distinguish the useful standalone MVP from the optional expert-community cycle and later public-product decision, and link to the full project description and interactive workspace.
- **REQ-015**: Derive client-facing planning scenarios only from explicit work-package hours and named rate inputs; show formulas, units, linked sources, and unresolved assumptions. Keep the price inputs visibly provisional when source pages or partner rates are unverified; do not imply a supplier quote or a success-shaped zero. Keep additional consulting separate and prevent duplicated TII charges.
- **REQ-019**: In the member cabinet, frame service interactions as local requests to add/change services, not live configuration; provide service-specific need/behavior prompts, preserve local draft restoration, and make no submission/approval claim. Center the request dialog safely in the viewport, keep its content scrollable, and provide a low-contrast 64px grid on both dark and light review surfaces.
- **REQ-020**: Make the proposal navigation step to `/proposal` visually prominent and readable at mobile widths, and describe the route as an MVP/optional-cycle offer rather than three client packages or a one-client pilot.
- **REQ-016**: In `src/components/MemberCabinet.tsx`, add an opt-in service-dependency graph using `PROPOSAL_FUNCTIONS` and their declared dependencies. Keep the expert search visible behind the cabinet, preserve all existing service-deck and local-draft behavior, and provide keyboard-accessible function selection and details.
- **REQ-017**: Optimize the client proposal, configuration cabinet, and dependency view mobile-first. At a 390px viewport, preserve readable controls and prevent page-level horizontal overflow; use a compact node picker, and replace the long service carousel with the dependency view while it is open.
- **REQ-018**: Remove the external War Room/World Monitor destination from the app navigation and current operational descriptions. Do not leave an unrelated external-navigation control in the live application shell.

## 3. Alternatives

- **ALT-001**: Reuse the home graph and its access-context payload. Rejected because it is coupled to unrelated identity/access behavior and global dark styling. Although the intended authorized review may need names and source-linked professional details, current data access does not establish partner authorization; this route therefore uses a separate pseudonymized surface. Public or uncontrolled access remains blocked pending a real authorization boundary.
- **ALT-002**: Implement server-side approvals and transmission now. Rejected because no shared authenticated backend or authorized submission destination is configured; a local draft/export is the only truthful operational state.
- **ALT-003**: Treat all client pricing as blank until final partner and supplier quotes exist. Replaced for the discussion draft by provisional low/base/high scenarios that are arithmetically derived from explicit WBS planning-hour assumptions and recorded but currently unverified rate inputs; show the derivation and caveats, never present the result as an approved quote.
- **ALT-004**: Use one blended international hourly rate. Rejected because source classes, currencies, and units differ; preserve each comparator's source unit and avoid unsupported currency normalization.
