# ISRM — delivery boundaries and acceptance addendum

30 October 2026 · Internal quotation clarification · Read with ISRM_Architecture_Clarification_EN.md

This addendum records the useful principles adopted from the reviewed five-block proposal draft. It does not introduce new client prices, hours caps, warranty periods, support commitments or accepted contract terms. Existing WBS hours remain planning assumptions before accepted reuse. The three core delivery/payment stages remain separate from decisions to commission optional workflows.

## 1. Establish the current workload

Agree a representative enquiry, nominated users, permitted sample and current operating approach. Measure time to a useful shortlist, time spent checking sources and repeated manual work. State the measurement conditions before proposing targets. Do not assume 40 hours per enquiry, a 30–40% miss rate, zero current yield, future conversion or £180,000–£280,000 savings.

## 2. Define included and separately assessed work

Return the named capabilities, users, data volumes, permitted sources, environments and connectors in the proposed release. Distinguish file import from wider legacy-system migration. The existing AI OSINT researcher adapter is already part of the planned core WBS; do not silently substitute LinkedIn sign-in as the only connector.

Assess additional connectors, migration, native mobile applications, model training and managed operations separately unless explicitly included in the agreed specification. Preserve the scope selected through the configurator, including tailored core contents. Identify shared dependencies once, and show hosting, licences, API/research usage, professional review and recurring support as distinct costs. An exclusion is not a commitment to supply the item in a later stage.

## 3. Define the change process

For a requested change, record the requirement and assess effects on outcomes, dependencies, acceptance checks, price and timetable before written agreement. Distinguish a changed requirement from a defect against the agreed specification. Explain responsibility for discovering and reporting data-quality and provider limitations; do not silently turn every such issue into extra billable work.

## 4. Define budget control according to the pricing model

Propose fixed-price outcomes, time-and-materials limits or another explicit model. Return the budget boundary, what counts towards it, reporting frequency, escalation point and decisions available if an overrun is forecast. Additional chargeable work requires written authorisation. State how dependent work is paused or rescheduled while a decision is pending.

Do not copy the draft's 320/80/40-hour limits. Its listed core, three packages and integration total 600 hours, not 520; the corresponding 80% point would be 480, not 416. Those figures are not approved limits. Any supplier risk allocation and treatment of unapproved hours must be explicitly priced and agreed.

## 5. Keep acceptance separate from warranty and ongoing support

For each core delivery stage, return measurable checks, test data, evidence, reviewer, review period, defect handling and acceptance record. Test ordinary search, dossier provenance/status, retained selection, permitted export, access boundaries and the named researcher adapter as applicable to the agreed release. Do not require invented membership counts, unexplained AI-generated relationships, a protected PDF, a consent mechanism or an immutable log unless the requirement and implementation are actually in scope.

Propose any warranty period with its start, covered defects, exclusions and relationship to acceptance. Define support hours, time zone, severity definitions, acknowledgement targets, restoration/resolution targets and third-party dependencies separately. Do not promise four-hour support or 24-hour resolution without a costed staffing arrangement.

Acceptance must not depend on completing a later warranty period. Do not combine a ten-business-day acceptance window with a requirement to finish thirty warranty days first, or introduce an indefinite warranty reset. Milestone evidence and payment conditions require the detailed agreement; clicking a scope discussion choice is not an order, acceptance of delivered work or approval of a final price.

## Reply requested from the architect/delivery team

- Included deliverables and dependencies, with existing outputs reused, adapted or replaced and the resulting remaining effort.
- Pricing model, inclusions, recurring costs and budget escalation procedure.
- Stage-specific acceptance matrix and proposed review/defect process.
- Separately costed warranty/support option and handover/exit arrangements.
- Questions that genuinely block a reliable detailed quotation.

Client-facing shorthand is maintained in src/lib/scope-delivery-notes.ts and included consistently in the scope disclosure, email summary, Markdown export and print document. These are proposed arrangements for discussion, not predetermined contract clauses.
