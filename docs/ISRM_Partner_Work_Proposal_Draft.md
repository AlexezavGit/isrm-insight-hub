## 1. Why this review is being requested

The ISRM work combines consulting, an expert-network application, and an operating pilot. The partner team needs to agree what work it is proposing, who owns each result, how the application work is separated from partner delivery, and which scope options should be presented later to the client.

The purpose of this document is to make the proposed work understandable and reviewable. It asks partners to review the work description, the accompanying cost models, and the remaining commercial decisions. The current whole-project planning source is `ISRM_Partner_Budget_Model_Draft.xlsx`; it contains partner-rate inputs and is for partner review, not client distribution. `ISRM_Partner_Cost_Model_Clean.xlsx` is a separate four-scope, three-partner-role input scaffold with a conditional graph/search replacement-value reference; it is not the whole-project budget or an approved client quote. ## 2. The proposed service in plain language

The proposed service helps an organization define a question or project need, identify relevant expertise in an existing network, form a purpose-specific group, and organize an agreed activity such as a discussion, research task, event, or project. The work can then produce an expert result, approved knowledge materials, and follow-up opportunities for contributors. The next cycle can start from the organization’s next question or identified expertise gap.

This describes a proposed operating loop, not a promise that participation, referrals, revenue, or impact will occur. Each activity depends on an agreed purpose, suitable data, permission to contact participants, and a human decision to proceed.

### Запропонований сервіс простими словами

Сервіс має допомагати організації визначити запит або потребу проєкту, знайти релевантну експертизу в наявній мережі, сформувати цільову групу й організувати погоджену діяльність — наприклад, обговорення, дослідницьке завдання, подію чи проєкт. Результатом може бути експертний матеріал, погоджені матеріали бази знань і подальші можливості для учасників. Наступний цикл може початися з нового запиту організації або виявленої прогалини експертизи.

Це опис запропонованого робочого циклу, а не обіцянка участі, рекомендацій, доходу чи впливу. Кожна діяльність залежить від погодженої мети, придатних даних, дозволу на контакт з учасниками та людського рішення про продовження.

## 5. Pricing boundaries — not three partner roles or four offer scopes

The later cost model will keep these layers separate:

1. **Conditional graph/search replacement-value reference.** The supporting model shows a £2,625 base-case benchmark in Scope 2, with an assumed range of £1,575–£3,675. Formula: assumed developer-days × cited UK market median day rate; low/base/high = 3/5/7 days × £525/day. The £525/day input is the UK Software Developer contractor median reported by [ITJobsWatch](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/contracts/uk/software%20developer.do) for the six months to 5 October 2026 (256 quoted daily rates; workbook source row `SRC-15`). The 3/5/7-day effort values are an explicit `[ASSUMPTION]`, not measured or sourced labor (workbook row `ASM-04`); the midpoint is not a quotation. Currency and period: one-time GBP benchmark, VAT excluded. The four scoped code files contain 564 physical lines (533 nonblank): `NetworkGraph.tsx` (239), `filters.tsx` (152), `network.functions.ts` (34), and `experts.server.ts` (139). These counts describe the inspected code footprint; they do not establish historic labor, ownership, a licence value, or a contractual right to reuse. This is not an approved client charge or partner payment. Any client-facing price and any internal author compensation require separate explicit agreement, and the line must be excluded if this exact reuse is already covered by an accepted contract. ITJobsWatch is a single Tier 4 market source, not an agreed tariff or supplier quote.
2. **Internal author allocation from the reuse fee.** This is a separate partner decision and is not added again to the client total. Its current model input remains excluded until the author, rights, amount, and allocation basis are agreed. It must not be treated as equal to the client-facing fee by default.
3. **Residual software work.** This covers only the software still required for the selected scope after the exact demonstration build, persistence, integrations, access rules, security, QA, and deployment boundaries are checked with the development team.
4. **Partner work.** This covers the deliverables owned by the three partner roles in Section 4, estimated separately from supplier implementation.

The four cost items do not map one-to-one to the three partner roles or four offer scopes. The base reuse reference is `5 developer-days × £525/day = £2,625`; the low/high cases are `3 × £525 = £1,575` and `7 × £525 = £3,675`. The day counts are an editable work-breakdown assumption, not measured or externally benchmarked effort. The recorded 9 days, 5 hours, and 27 minutes between the first and latest relevant file edits is elapsed calendar time and includes idle periods; it is not billed labor. The model retains editable assumptions, formulas, and source labels. It does not import old package totals or treat assumptions as partner-agreed rates, measured effort, supplier quotations, or final client prices.
