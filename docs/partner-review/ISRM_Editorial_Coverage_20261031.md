## Figures and claims deliberately not adopted

- 178 supplied records and 45 `is_verified` flags are data facts, not proof of 45 verified members, consent, suitability or willingness. The presentation labels this distinction.
- No evidence supports 0% current yield, a 12% conversion target, promised mandate income, trained-model status, immutable audit storage or encrypted export as completed demo functionality.
- 78/640 is **12.19%**, not 28%. The proposed 78 hours and 50 reusable hours were not accepted as measured estimates. Reuse must be assessed component by component.
- 320 + 3 × 80 + 40 is **600 hours**, not 520. Neither the erroneous total nor the individual caps were accepted as commercial terms.
- A 500-profile threshold is not a general basis for deciding whether model training is appropriate. No such threshold was adopted.
- API access, LinkedIn review times, integration entitlements and third-party commitments must be verified for a named connection. No fixed timeline was inferred from a logo.
- An acceptance deadline cannot simultaneously require a later warranty to have finished. These are separate decisions in the revised delivery notes.
- No unsupported Edelman interpretation or universal two-font psychological rule was used. Two presentation families are a design choice.

The six slides now keep one originating enquiry and separate jobs: research output as a resource; an actual sample and human review; services around people; chapter coordination and shared rules; a participation cycle to test; a clear next decision. The partner print pack explains the same sequence and includes the technical and financial discussion.
