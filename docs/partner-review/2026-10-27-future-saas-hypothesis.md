Scenario: target 250 paying seats, assumed reach-to-registration 5%, registration-to-paid 14%. Required distinct incoming people = 250 / (0.05 × 0.14) = 35,714.29 (round up to 35,715 for at least 250 expected seats). At £25–35/month, 250 retained full-year paying seats yield £75,000–105,000 annualised recurring gross revenue, before discounts, taxes, payment costs or churn.

30,000 reach → 1,500 registrations → 210 expected paid seats. 40,000 → 2,000 → 280. This demonstrates arithmetic sensitivity; it does not prove demand, feasible conversion or economic break-even. The 5% and 14% values are assumptions supplied by the user. No evidence supporting the claimed sector benchmark has been checked in this batch. Costs, retention/churn, acquisition spend, support, licensing, infrastructure, delivery margin and timing are necessary to calculate break-even.
